PhD positions for Fall 2027

We are recruiting PhD students to start in Fall 2027, when Octopus Lab opens at UT Dallas. If you are curious about how the brain recovers after stroke and want hands-on experience with brain stimulation and neuroimaging, we would like to hear from you.

You might be a good fit if you have

  • A background in speech-language pathology, neuroscience, psychology, cognitive science, or linguistics.
  • Interest in aphasia, stroke rehabilitation, or brain plasticity.
  • Curiosity about methods. Prior experience with TMS, tDCS, EEG, MRI or programming is welcome but not required.

What you will work on

  • Clinical studies with people living with aphasia, from design to publication.
  • rTMS, tDCS, EEG and fMRI analysis, including lesion and connectivity mapping.
  • Research questions shaped with clinicians, so the work reaches real therapy.

Students apply through the Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences PhD program in the School of Behavioral and Brain Sciences. Full-time PhD students are considered for research assistant stipends, tuition scholarships and health insurance.

Application deadline

Nov 15, 2026

  1. Email me first with your CV and a few lines on your interests, so we can talk before you apply.
  2. Apply through the UT Dallas graduate portal.
